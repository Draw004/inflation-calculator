How to use this tool

Three ideas to keep separate

Today's money

What your current amount can buy now. This is the easiest reference point when thinking about lifestyle or purchasing power.

Future nominal money

The larger number of currency units you may need later if prices rise. It is not automatically “more wealth”; part of the increase may simply reflect inflation.
